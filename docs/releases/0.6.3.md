### What's fixed

- **Races run at 60 FPS again.** Since 0.6.0 many races dropped to 24-45 FPS in slow motion while menus stayed at 60, most visibly in Grand Prix and VS races with 12 racers. Every draw re-read the upscaler and frame-generation settings from the system environment, which is slow on Windows, even with those features off. Each setting is now read once: a 12-racer Grand Prix on an RX 580 went from 39 to 60 FPS on average ([#133](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/133)). Run **Update the build** once after updating.
- **Linux: the launcher recognizes a finished build again.** In 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 two build capabilities were read from a Windows DLL, so on Linux the build always looked outdated: additional content and rooms stayed locked even though solo play worked ([#128](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/128)).
- **Linux: Steam Controller on Wayland.** When Steam starts MK8-online in a Wayland session, the game now runs through XWayland, so Steam follows the game window and the controller leaves desktop mode ([#131](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/131)). Setting `SDL_VIDEODRIVER` yourself still takes precedence.
- **No more closing while a race loads with "completed occlusion queries unavailable".** When the graphics driver did not return the results of these visibility checks, the game stopped on purpose. It now treats them as visible and keeps running ([#127](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/127)).

Everything from [0.6.0](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/releases/tag/v0.6.0) and [0.6.1](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/releases/tag/v0.6.1) is included. 0.6.3 replaces 0.6.2, which was withdrawn to include the frame-rate fix.

Bring your own game files, update 4.0.1 and console keys. Each package has a companion SHA-256. Include your GPU, driver, mode, track and a **Support → Create report** ZIP when reporting an issue. ### O que foi corrigido

- **As corridas voltam a rodar a 60 FPS.** Desde a 0.6.0, muitas corridas caíam para 24-45 FPS em câmera lenta, enquanto os menus ficavam a 60, principalmente no Grand Prix e no VS com 12 pilotos. Todo draw relia no ambiente do sistema as opções do upscaler e da geração de quadros, o que é lento no Windows, mesmo com esses recursos desligados. Agora cada opção é lida uma vez: um Grand Prix com 12 pilotos numa RX 580 passou de 39 para 60 FPS em média ([#133](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/133)). Rode **Atualizar a build** uma vez depois de atualizar.
- **Linux: o launcher volta a reconhecer a build concluída.** Na 0.6.0 e na 0.6.1, duas capacidades da build eram lidas de uma DLL do Windows, e no Linux a build sempre parecia desatualizada: o conteúdo adicional e as salas ficavam bloqueados, embora o jogo solo funcionasse ([#128](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/128)).
- **Linux: Steam Controller no Wayland.** Quando a Steam abre o MK8-online numa sessão Wayland, o jogo passa a rodar pelo XWayland. Assim a Steam acompanha a janela do jogo e o controle sai do modo desktop ([#131](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/131)). Se você definir `SDL_VIDEODRIVER`, a sua escolha continua valendo.
- **O jogo não fecha mais ao carregar uma corrida com "completed occlusion queries unavailable".** Quando o driver de vídeo não devolvia os resultados dessas verificações de visibilidade, o jogo parava de propósito. Agora elas contam como visíveis e o jogo continua ([#127](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/issues/127)).

Tudo da [0.6.0](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/releases/tag/v0.6.0) e da [0.6.1](https://github.com/Vnnc1/MK8-online/releases/tag/v0.6.1) continua incluído. A 0.6.3 substitui a 0.6.2, retirada para incluir a correção de FPS.

Use seus próprios arquivos do jogo, atualização 4.0.1 e chaves do console. Cada pacote acompanha um SHA-256. Ao relatar um erro, inclua GPU, driver, modo, pista e o ZIP de **Suporte → Gerar relatório**.
