## Vulkan depth bias — 2026-11-18

Consulted the [Khronos depth bias specification](https://docs.vulkan.org/spec/latest/chapters/primsrast.html#primsrast-depthbias): the slope term depends on the largest depth gradient in framebuffer coordinates; the constant term is separate. The original compensation takes the target's real uniform scale into account and preserves the other terms. The private replay also follows the [render pass store rules](https://docs.vulkan.org/spec/latest/chapters/renderpass.html#renderpass-store-operations) to synchronize the depth image. No code from these pages was copied; commercial data stays private and external results do not qualify this build.
