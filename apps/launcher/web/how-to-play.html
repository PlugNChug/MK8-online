How to Play

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe · local guide

These are the controller buttons shown by the game. To check your PC keyboard or controller mappings, open F1 → Controls in the game.

Basic controls

Left stick
Steer your kart.
A
Accelerate.
B
Brake; reverse when stopped.
R
Hop, drift, and perform a ramp trick.
L
Use an item; hold to trail a banana or shell behind you.
X
Look behind.
+
Pause the race.

Driving and boosts

Keep accelerating through a turn and hold R to drift. Steer to control the curve, then release R to use a charged Mini-Turbo. Press R as you leave a ramp for a trick boost.

Dash panels boost your kart. Rough ground slows it down. Brake before a difficult corner, especially in 200cc.

Using items

Drive through item boxes to fill your item slots. Press L to use the next item. Hold an item behind you for protection when the item allows it. Steer forward or backward while using an item to aim, when supported.

Collect coins to increase your speed, up to ten coins.

Driving assists

Smart Steering helps keep your kart on the course. Auto-Accelerate holds the throttle for you. Check the assist icons in kart selection or the pause menu, and use the buttons shown there to change them. Tilt controls steer by controller motion when supported.

Choosing a mode

Grand Prix runs a four-race cup. Time Trials lets you practise a course and save a ghost. VS Race lets you choose race rules. Battle uses items and arena objectives. Mario Kart TV replays recent races.

Multiplayer support in MK8-online is experimental.
